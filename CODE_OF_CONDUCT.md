# Code of Conduct

**GBLOXKOS LLC** · <support@gbloxkos.io>

---

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
GBLOXKOS AI DARKWEB WIZARD X a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our standards

**Expected behaviour**

- Be respectful, patient and constructive — especially with newcomers.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before criticising.
- Give and accept actionable, specific feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for defensive security practice.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experience.

**Unacceptable behaviour**

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate misreporting a vulnerability, or pressuring anyone to disclose one.
- Sustained disruption, spam, or off-topic advocacy.
- Any conduct that facilitates illegal harm.

---

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits, and any space where someone represents the project — and when an
individual officially represents GBLOXKOS LLC in public.

---

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **<support@gbloxkos.io>**. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. You may report anonymously.

Report **vulnerabilities** through the private channel in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead — never as a public issue.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers responsible for enforcement will:

1. Issue a private written warning explaining the violation.
2. On a second or severe violation, issue a temporary ban.
3. On repeated or severe violations, issue a permanent ban.

Any maintainer found abusing their authority should be reported to GBLOXKOS
LLC leadership and will face the same consequences as any other participant.
